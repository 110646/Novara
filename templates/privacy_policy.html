Novara Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2025
Last Updated: August 10, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Novara ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our automated research professor matching service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information from Google Sign-On (SSO)

When you authenticate using Google SSO, we collect:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Your full name as provided by Google
  • Your Google profile picture (if available)
  • Unique Google account identifier

2.2 Profile Information You Provide

  • University or educational institution
  • Academic major or field of study
  • Research interests and keywords
  • Academic level (undergraduate, graduate, etc.)
  • Contact preferences

2.3 Email and Communication Data

  • Email content you compose or customize using our templates
  • Records of emails sent through our platform
  • Email delivery status and basic engagement metrics
  • Correspondence with professors facilitated through our service

2.4 Usage and Technical Information

  • IP address and device information
  • Browser type and version
  • Platform usage patterns and feature interactions
  • Error logs and performance data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Authenticate your identity and provide secure access
  • Match you with relevant research professors based on your interests
  • Generate and send personalized outreach emails on your behalf
  • Track the status and success of your email campaigns
  • Improve our matching algorithms and service quality
  • Provide customer support and technical assistance
  • Send service-related notifications and updates
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

4. Email Sending Authorization

4.1 Gmail API Integration

By using Novara, you explicitly authorize us to:

  • Send emails through your Gmail account using the Gmail API
  • Access your Gmail account solely for the purpose of sending research outreach emails
  • Store temporary access tokens necessary for email delivery

4.2 Email Content and Responsibility

  • All emails are sent from your personal Gmail account
  • You maintain full control over email content before sending
  • You are responsible for the accuracy and appropriateness of email content
  • We provide templates and suggestions but do not guarantee professor responses

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

5.1 With Your Consent

  • When you explicitly authorize us to share specific information
  • When facilitating introductions between you and professors

5.2 Service Providers

  • Third-party services that help us operate our platform (hosting, analytics, email delivery)
  • These providers are contractually bound to protect your information

5.3 Legal Requirements

  • When required by law, court order, or legal process
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users
  • In connection with legal proceedings or investigations

5.4 Business Transfers

  • In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • Your information may be transferred as part of the transaction

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security audits and monitoring
  • Limited access to personal information by authorized personnel
  • Compliance with Google's security requirements for API usage

7. Data Retention

  • Account information: Retained while your account is active
  • Email records: Stored for up to 2 years for service improvement
  • Usage analytics: Retained for up to 1 year in aggregated form
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Account Management

  • Update your profile information at any time
  • Control which professors receive your outreach emails
  • Manage your email preferences and frequency

8.2 Data Rights

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing (may limit service functionality)
  • Export your data in a portable format

8.3 Communication Preferences

  • Opt out of promotional emails
  • Customize notification settings
  • Manage email template preferences

9. Third-Party Services

Our platform integrates with:

  • Google Services (Authentication, Gmail API, Google Scholar)
  • Analytics providers for service improvement
  • Email delivery services for system notifications

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers.

11. Children's Privacy

Novara is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or platform notification. Your continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Information

For privacy-related questions or requests:

  • Email: privacy@researchify.com
  • Address: [Your Business Address]

Refund Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2025
Last Updated: August 10, 2025

1. No-Refund Policy

Novara operates under a no-refund policy for all services. This policy applies to all subscription fees, one-time payments, and premium features. All sales are final.

2. Service Model Explanation

Our service provides:

  • Automated matching with research professors
  • Personalized email template generation
  • Email sending through your Gmail account
  • Campaign tracking and analytics
  • Access to our professor database

Given the immediate delivery of digital services and the resources invested in each user interaction, we cannot offer monetary refunds.

3. Free Consultation Service

In lieu of refunds, we offer a complimentary consultation service under the following conditions:

3.1 Eligibility Requirements

  • You must have completed 2 full email cycles through our platform
  • Total of 600 emails sent (300 emails per cycle)
  • Account must be in good standing with no policy violations
  • Consultation must be requested within 30 days of completing the 600-email milestone

3.2 Consultation Services Include

  • One-on-one strategy session (45-60 minutes) with our research outreach specialist
  • Personalized email template optimization based on your specific field
  • Professor selection strategy review and recommendations
  • Response rate analysis and improvement suggestions
  • Academic networking guidance tailored to your research interests
  • Follow-up email sequences and best practices training

3.3 Consultation Scheduling

  • Sessions are conducted via video call (Zoom, Google Meet, or similar)
  • Available during business hours (Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM EST)
  • Must be scheduled within 60 days of eligibility
  • Rescheduling allowed up to 24 hours in advance

4. Service Guarantee

While we cannot offer refunds, we guarantee:

4.1 Platform Availability

  • 99.5% uptime commitment
  • Technical support for platform issues
  • Regular feature updates and improvements

4.2 Email Delivery

  • Best-effort delivery through Gmail API
  • Delivery status tracking and reporting
  • Technical support for email-related issues

4.3 Data Accuracy

  • Regularly updated professor database
  • Accurate contact information (minimum 95% accuracy rate)
  • Prompt corrections when inaccuracies are reported

5. Exceptional Circumstances

In rare cases involving:

  • Significant platform downtime (over 72 consecutive hours)
  • Data breach affecting your personal information
  • Fundamental service unavailability due to our negligence

We may consider service credits or extended access periods rather than monetary refunds.

6. Billing Issues

For billing disputes or payment errors:

  • Contact support within 30 days of the charge
  • Provide detailed information about the billing issue
  • We will investigate and resolve legitimate billing errors promptly

7. Account Termination

7.1 Voluntary Cancellation

  • You may cancel your account at any time
  • Access continues until the end of your billing period
  • No refunds for unused portions of service periods

7.2 Policy Violations

  • Accounts terminated for policy violations forfeit all paid services
  • No refunds or consultations provided in cases of violation
  • Users are responsible for ensuring compliance with our terms

8. Third-Party Payment Processors

  • Payments processed through secure third-party providers
  • Chargebacks may result in account suspension
  • Contact us before initiating payment disputes

9. Contact for Policy Questions

For questions about our refund policy or to request your consultation:

  • Email: helpnovaraco@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: "Refund Policy Inquiry" or "Consultation Request"

10. Policy Updates

This refund policy may be updated periodically. Users will be notified of material changes via email. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of policy updates.

By using Novara, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to both our Privacy Policy and Refund Policy.
