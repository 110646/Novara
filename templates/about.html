About Novara

Novara is an automated platform that connects students with research opportunities and internships, submits applications on your behalf, conducts outreach to professors and hiring managers, and provides career advancement tools and analytics. Novara streamlines the process of finding and applying for research and internship positions, making it easier for students to connect with the right opportunities and advance their careers.

  • Automated matching with research and internship opportunities
  • Application submissions and outreach on your behalf
  • Personalized analytics and career guidance
  • Secure handling of your data and documents

Our mission is to empower students to achieve their academic and professional goals with less hassle and more success.
