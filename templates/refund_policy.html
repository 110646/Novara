Novara Refund Policy

Effective Date: July 21, 2025

At Novara, we strive to provide valuable and effective services to students and professionals seeking research opportunities, internships, and career advancement. We understand that circumstances may change, and we want to ensure a fair and transparent refund process.

1. Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds may be considered under the following conditions:

  • You were charged in error.
  • You cancel a subscription within 7 days of the initial purchase and have not yet used any premium services (e.g., outreach, auto-application submissions).
  • The service failed to deliver the promised functionality due to a system error or service outage on our end.

2. Non-Refundable Services

Refunds will not be issued for:

  • Any service that has been used in part or in full (e.g., outreach emails sent, applications submitted).
  • Subscription renewals if not canceled prior to the billing date.
  • One-time payments tied to completed tasks or non-reversible actions.

3. Subscription Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account dashboard. Canceling will stop future billing, but no refund will be issued for the current billing period unless eligible under section 1.

4. Requesting a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us at support@novara.ai within 7 days of the charge. Include your account email, the reason for your request, and any relevant screenshots or documentation.

We review all refund requests on a case-by-case basis. Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

For questions about our refund policy, contact us at:
