Novara Refund Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2025
Last Updated: August 10, 2025

1. No-Refund Policy

Novara operates under a no-refund policy for all services. This policy applies to all subscription fees, one-time payments, and premium features. All sales are final.

2. Service Model Explanation

Our service provides:

  • Automated matching with research professors
  • Personalized email template generation
  • Email sending through your Gmail account
  • Campaign tracking and analytics
  • Access to our professor database

Given the immediate delivery of digital services and the resources invested in each user interaction, we cannot offer monetary refunds.

3. Free Consultation Service

In lieu of refunds, we offer a complimentary consultation service under the following conditions:

3.1 Eligibility Requirements

  • You must have completed 2 full email cycles through our platform
  • Total of 600 emails sent (300 emails per cycle)
  • Account must be in good standing with no policy violations
  • Consultation must be requested within 30 days of completing the 600-email milestone

3.2 Consultation Services Include

  • One-on-one strategy session (45-60 minutes) with our research outreach specialist
  • Personalized email template optimization based on your specific field
  • Professor selection strategy review and recommendations
  • Response rate analysis and improvement suggestions
  • Academic networking guidance tailored to your research interests
  • Follow-up email sequences and best practices training

3.3 Consultation Scheduling

  • Sessions are conducted via video call (Zoom, Google Meet, or similar)
  • Available during business hours (Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM EST)
  • Must be scheduled within 60 days of eligibility
  • Rescheduling allowed up to 24 hours in advance

4. Service Guarantee

While we cannot offer refunds, we guarantee:

4.1 Platform Availability

  • 99.5% uptime commitment
  • Technical support for platform issues
  • Regular feature updates and improvements

4.2 Email Delivery

  • Best-effort delivery through Gmail API
  • Delivery status tracking and reporting
  • Technical support for email-related issues

4.3 Data Accuracy

  • Regularly updated professor database
  • Accurate contact information (minimum 95% accuracy rate)
  • Prompt corrections when inaccuracies are reported

5. Exceptional Circumstances

In rare cases involving:

  • Significant platform downtime (over 72 consecutive hours)
  • Data breach affecting your personal information
  • Fundamental service unavailability due to our negligence

We may consider service credits or extended access periods rather than monetary refunds.

6. Billing Issues

For billing disputes or payment errors:

  • Contact support within 30 days of the charge
  • Provide detailed information about the billing issue
  • We will investigate and resolve legitimate billing errors promptly

7. Account Termination

7.1 Voluntary Cancellation

  • You may cancel your account at any time
  • Access continues until the end of your billing period
  • No refunds for unused portions of service periods

7.2 Policy Violations

  • Accounts terminated for policy violations forfeit all paid services
  • No refunds or consultations provided in cases of violation
  • Users are responsible for ensuring compliance with our terms

8. Third-Party Payment Processors

  • Payments processed through secure third-party providers
  • Chargebacks may result in account suspension
  • Contact us before initiating payment disputes

9. Contact for Policy Questions

For questions about our refund policy or to request your consultation:

  • Email: helpnovaraco@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: "Refund Policy Inquiry" or "Consultation Request"

10. Policy Updates

This refund policy may be updated periodically. Users will be notified of material changes via email. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of policy updates.

By using Novara, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to our Refund Policy.
